## Visual direction
- Cinematic, editorial, luxury automotive. Obsidian #090a0c, deep red #e82937, warm white.
- Oversized Swiss typography, asymmetrical layouts, real photography, generous negative space, precise microinteractions.
- Scroll choreography inspired by award-winning automotive studios, not generic cards/templates.
- Always prioritize brand authenticity over gratuitous effects.

- Core animated story feels extraordinary and scroll scrubbing is smooth.
- Every important action has a functioning demo-safe path.

- Production Vercel deployment READY; verify screenshots on mobile and desktop.
